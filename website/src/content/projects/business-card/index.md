A business card design I created for myself. I got inspired form my playthrough of Persona 5 and I thought that making a 'calling card' for myself would be a fun project. The project was quite fast and interesting and I am still using the design to this day. I still have some from the original batch!

I used Adobe Photoshop for sketching the design and coming up with the layout. I then used Adobe Illustrator to create the actual vector graphics of the card. I used CMYK colors and tried to make sure that they would be bright enough for the print. In the next batch I will need to make it a bit more bright though.

Finally I sent the pdf files with the design and 3mm bleed to a printing company that I found online. The end result was very nice and the paper felt premium quality. There were no issues with the print process.
