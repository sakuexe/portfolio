This site is a personal portfolio. It has no analytics, no ads and no tracking
cookies. The only personal data it processes is what is needed to serve the
pages, keep spam out of the contact form, and answer the messages you send.

## Who is responsible

Saku Karttunen, Finland. For anything about this policy or your data, email
<saku.karttunen@proton.me>.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
To serve the pages and protect them against abuse, Cloudflare processes
technical data about each request, such as your IP address, browser and the
page you asked for. I don't receive or keep these request logs myself.

## Spam protection (Cloudflare Turnstile)

The contact form on the home page is protected by
[Cloudflare Turnstile](https://www.cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy/).
Turnstile is only loaded once you start filling in the form; visitors who
don't use the form never contact it. It then checks in the background that
you're human. To do that, it sends Cloudflare your IP address and information
about your browser and device. Turnstile doesn't set tracking cookies, and
Cloudflare doesn't use this data for advertising.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest in keeping automated spam out of the
form (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

## Contact form

When you send the form, I receive your name, email address, subject and
message by email, so that I can reply to you. The legal basis is my legitimate
interest in answering the messages people send me, or steps you ask for
before we make an agreement (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f) and (b)).

- The message is delivered through [Scaleway](https://www.scaleway.com/en/privacy-policy/)'s
  email service (servers in the EU) to my personal inbox.
- The site keeps no copy in a database.
- If sending fails or the form is flagged as spam, the server log records a
  shortened form of your email address (for example `sa***@gmail.com`) and the
  lengths of the fields, but not your name or message. These logs are kept by
  Cloudflare for up to 7 days.
- I keep messages for as long as needed to handle the conversation. Messages
  that don't lead anywhere are deleted within a year. If they lead to working
  together, they're kept as long as that relationship and any legal
  obligations require. You can ask me to delete them at any time.

## Transfers outside the EU

Cloudflare is a US company. Transfers to it are covered by the EU–US Data
Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask me to correct or delete it, and
object to its processing. Email me at the address above. If you think your
data has been handled wrongly, you can also complain to the Finnish
[Data Protection Ombudsman](https://tietosuoja.fi/en).
